Ben Nix-Bradley

BIO

Ben Nix-Bradley is a Composer and Computer Artist from Los Angeles, California. In 2023, he graduated with honors from California State University, Northridge, earning a Bachelor of Music in Media and Commercial Music Composition. He has worked as a professional composer for the past decade, collaborating on award-winning independent films and broadcast TV series. With over two decades of experience as a freelance digital artist specializing in interactive software and system design, Ben is an expert at adapting to diverse environments and contributing creative solutions to unique situations. As an educator, he has experience utilizing that adaptability to meet the needs and approaches of a wide range of students in the creative field. His experience as a self-starter accommodates students at their individual skill level with an understanding that we approach every day with different levels of personal comfort and skill.
